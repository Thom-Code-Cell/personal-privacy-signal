# Open research governance — initial model

TH Analytica / Thomas Hullin initiated PPS/PPS+. Origin is preserved; independent
research, criticism and implementations are welcome under existing rights.
Participation does not imply endorsement, employment, partnership or adoption.

Initial repository owner and acting maintainer: **Thom-Code-Cell (Thomas Hullin)**.
No university, manufacturer or other person is listed as a partner or maintainer
without their agreement. Maintainer nominations use a public PR documenting
contributions, review ability, availability, conflicts and explicit acceptance.
Owner grants only necessary permissions; no automatic admin access. Removal or
resignation is recorded, with access revoked and responsibilities reassigned.

Decisions use public issues/PRs, evidence and a reasonable review window (normally
7 days). Record alternatives, dissent, decision maker and rationale. Seek consensus;
the acting maintainer resolves routine deadlocks with reasons. Independent forks
remain possible under the applicable license. No voting constituency is invented.
For disputed security/protocol claims seek a non-author reviewer; if unavailable,
mark independent review pending rather than approve one's own research evidence.

Protocol/policy changes must identify editorial, compatible or breaking scope,
provide positive/negative vectors and update version/change notes. Frozen files
are not silently redefined. Emergency fixes may shorten review with a recorded
reason and follow-up review. Keep security details private until coordinated.

Licenses, patent commitments, rights transfers, paid obligations, releases,
external announcements and endorsements require explicit rights-holder/owner
approval. Contributions do not transfer ownership or authorise relicensing.
Maintainers check provenance before acceptance. No new legal terms in this PR.
Merges, releases and deployments remain separate decisions; CI is not approval.

Roadmap: independent parser replication → threat/privacy review → bounded T1
research → independently validated binding → supported device/enforcement trials.
Every stage needs a named consenting owner and measured exit criteria; see the
[research roadmap](docs/research-v1/07-roadmap.md). Unassigned work is an invitation,
not a delivery promise. Funding and material conflicts must be disclosed.
