# Invitation to independent PPS / PPS+ research

TH Analytica initiated PPS to explore how people can express privacy preferences
to Physical-AI systems. We invite developers, universities, manufacturers,
accessibility specialists and interested individuals to investigate independently.
No current manufacturer partnership, certification or standards endorsement is claimed.

Useful bounded contributions:
- Developers: a second T0 parser in another language, with malformed-input vectors.
- Universities: independent binding/room/relay methodology and negative results.
- Hardware makers: documented SDK feasibility and supported enforcement boundaries,
  with exact model/firmware and public evidence; no proprietary SDK uploads.
- Everyone: accessible explanations, translations, threat reviews and reproducibility.

Start with README and CONTRIBUTING, then open a research proposal in Issues or a
question in Discussions. A small written scope should state output, independence,
data/rights handling, time and any costs before collaboration. Do not collect
personal data or contact participants on behalf of this project without approval.
Existing licensing applies; the future license decision is still open. No new
license, NDA, commitment, exclusivity or paid engagement is created by this invitation.

The [earlier partner dossier](../research-v1/06-partners.md) is prospective research
planning, not a closed partner list. This invitation is published in-repository
only; it has not been sent to any person, institution or manufacturer.
