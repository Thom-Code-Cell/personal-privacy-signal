# Licensing decision brief — NOT a license grant

Decision pending, 2026-10-21. LICENSE.md is unchanged. Do not interpret this brief
as applying Apache, CC or a noncommercial restriction to any existing material.

## Existing position

The published LICENSE.md specifies the W3C Software and Document License (2023)
for public v0.1 materials. Its linked text permits copying, modification and
distribution for any purpose, subject to notice conditions, and reserves trademarks.
Thus the current baseline is not a noncommercial-only publication. GitHub reports
NOASSERTION for machine recognition; that does not mean no license exists.
Prior recipients' rights cannot simply be erased by replacing a file. The record
does not establish all upstream ownership or patent rights. No patent search or
freedom-to-operate conclusion has been made.

## Preferred future option: Apache-2.0

For a broad developer/university/manufacturer ecosystem, prefer an explicitly
approved Apache-2.0 grant for rights-cleared future code and specifications,
with retained original notices and an explicit scope/migration inventory.
It is OSI approved and permits commercial use. Its contributor patent grant is
limited to necessarily infringed claims and includes patent-litigation termination;
that is a substantive rights decision requiring authorised holders' agreement.
It does not license trademarks or certify products. Keep documentation under the
existing terms unless a separate documentation choice is explicitly approved.
Keeping the existing W3C terms unchanged is also a viable lowest-change option;
OSI approval is not asserted here for that exact 2023 text.

## If noncommercial restriction is intended

For future, separately owned explanatory documents, CC BY-NC 4.0 is an option to
review. It permits adaptations with attribution but restricts commercial use;
commercial advantage is broader than simply selling copies. It is not OSI open
source, complicates manufacturer and mixed-funded university participation, and
CC advises against using its licenses for software or NC for scientific databases.
For future software, obtain a reviewed source-available research license defining
research, commercial use, redistribution, patents and commercial permissions;
no improvised NC clause or CC-on-code is proposed. Existing W3C material and
third-party rights must remain separately identified. NC cannot retroactively
make already permitted uses prohibited.

## Approval needed before any change

Record: (1) commercial reuse yes/no; (2) exact files and future versions; (3)
authorised rights holders including employers and contributors; (4) patent and
notice obligations; (5) exact proposed text and effective commit; (6) migration
and preservation of existing rights. Do not merge a rights change without this
record. An open research invitation is not a new license or patent pledge.
