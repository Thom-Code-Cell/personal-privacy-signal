# Funding and sustainable participation

The public-interest research vision does not assert registered charitable status,
tax deductibility, secured funding or a nonprofit legal entity. No funder, grant,
sponsor agreement, payment destination or compensation entitlement is established.

| Possible route | Scope | Conditions before commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Voluntary sponsorship | tooling, accessibility, independent replication | agreed recipient/budget; disclose conflicts; no purchased conclusions |
| Competitive research grants | university collaboration and security/privacy evaluation | verify live call, eligibility, institution, budget, ethics and IP terms |
| Paid consulting | training, architecture or threat review | written deliverables, price, scope and liability; no certification promise |
| Integration projects | supported-device prototypes and test tooling | SDK rights, device access, privacy gates and measurable acceptance |
| In-kind work | translation, review or lab time | consent/provenance; no assumed partnership or governance privilege |

An idea, citation, public repository or future standard adoption creates no
automatic payment or royalties. Existing permitted use does not become chargeable
through this file. Independent research remains open under applicable terms;
sponsors receive no automatic veto or exclusive rights. Disclose paid work and
distinguish it from independent verification.

Use Discussions for a non-sensitive expression of interest; arrange confidential
contract processes separately with a consenting maintainer. No bank details,
personal addresses or unpublished applications in public threads. No fundraising
account, paid service, grant submission or GitHub Sponsors setup is activated.
